Where the revenue leaks

Six gaps between traffic and repeat orders

Leak 01

The two biggest DTC levers are missing entirely

There is no dedicated email and SMS lifecycle platform on the store, and your VIP popup captures an email but not a phone number. For supplement brands, email and SMS together routinely drive 20 to 30 percent of total revenue. Right now you are collecting emails into what is almost certainly a thin default setup, with no real welcome series, no browse or cart recovery beyond the platform basics, no post-purchase, and no winback. This is the single largest gap and the fastest money.

This one fix builds the entire revenue machine everything else feeds
Leak 02

The reorder is being left to memory

Supplements run out on a schedule. That reorder should be prompted the week before a customer hits empty, with a one-tap path back to subscribe-and-save. Subscriptions exist on your site but they are not being pushed hard at the right moments, and there is no replenishment reminder driving the cycle. In a consumable category, the reorder is the whole business, and right now it depends on the customer remembering.

Automated replenishment turns one-time buyers into recurring revenue
Leak 03

Your best salesperson is not online

What wins in the shop is a person who knows exactly which product fits your goal. Online, a customer picking between a dozen products gets no guidance, so they stall or bounce. A guided product finder and a 24/7 advisor that asks the goal, recommends the right stack, and captures the contact recreates your in-store superpower on every visit, at 2am, without adding staff.

Recreate the knowledgeable-staff experience that already sells for you
Leak 04

Your online proof lags your real reputation

Your in-store and local reputation is excellent, but your product pages carry only a handful of reviews, and there appear to be two review apps installed at once. Happy customers are not being asked at the right moment, so the proof never builds and shoppers comparing you to a competitor see fewer reviews than your reputation deserves. Cleaning up the stack and automating review requests fixes this fast.

Make your online proof match the reputation you have already earned
Leak 05

The ambassador program runs on manual effort

The program exists, which means you already believe in it. But onboarding new ambassadors, collecting their content, and keeping them active is hands-on work. Automating the activation and follow-up turns a scattered roster into a steady stream of content and referrals without eating your time.

Turn a manual roster into an automated referral engine
Leak 06

The store greets visitors with friction

On load, a cookie banner and the VIP popup stack up before a visitor has seen a thing. Capture is good, but timing and sequencing matter. Earning attention first, then asking for the email and the phone number, lifts both signup rate and the quality of the list you build.

Same capture, less bounce, and a phone number worth having
