Discovery, get these numbers

Questions that unlock the sizing

How much traffic is the site doing a month, roughly?
Sizes every leak. Even a rough range works.
Of the people who hit that signup popup, what share actually sign up, and what happens to them after?
Confirms the capture-then-drop gap and quantifies it.
When someone signs up or leaves a full cart and does not buy, does anything reach out to them automatically right now?

Sizes the retention and reactivation engine.

Who handles wholesale applications and affiliate signups, and how fast do they get a response?
Speed-to-lead on partners. Often manual and slow.

Sizes the 24/7 concierge value and after-hours loss.
What are your hard lines on messaging language for compliance?
