Where demand turns into repeat orders, or does not

The path a buyer actually takes

Following a customer from first click to the reorder that should follow. Green is working, amber is where revenue leaks today, blue is the system that closes it.

Working
Leak point
System we build

1. Lands on the site

A cookie banner and the VIP popup fire at once on load, before the visitor has seen the brand or a product.

Leak

Friction stacks up before value, and the popup captures an email but no phone number.

System

Sequence the capture to earn attention first, and add phone capture so SMS becomes possible.

2. Browses products

Clean Shopify store, clear catalog, subscribe-and-save available, real brand identity.

Working. The storefront itself is a solid asset.

3. Unsure which product fits their goal

A shopper choosing between a dozen products gets no guidance online, unlike in the store.

Leak

No guided product finder or advisor, so undecided buyers stall or bounce.

System

24/7 goal-based advisor that recommends the right stack and captures the contact.

4. Adds to cart, then leaves

The most common path in DTC. The window to win them back is measured in hours.

Leak

No dedicated lifecycle platform, so recovery is limited to platform defaults, and no SMS at all.

System

Email and SMS cart and checkout recovery that fires fast across both channels.

5. Buys and gets the order

Checkout and fulfillment work. The transaction is solid.

Working. Fulfillment is not the problem.

6. Runs out, could reorder

Consumable supplements empty on a schedule. The reorder is the whole business.

Leak

No replenishment reminder and a soft subscription push, so the reorder depends on memory.

System

Replenishment engine that reminds before empty with a one-tap path to reorder or subscribe.

7. Would vouch, barely asked

Strong local reputation, but only a handful of online reviews and two review apps installed at once.

Leak

Reviews are not requested systematically after purchase, so online proof lags the real reputation.

System

Reputation engine that asks at the right moment, and a cleaned-up review setup.

Confirm the numbers behind each leak on the call. Amber points are hypotheses from a public review, sized precisely against Stax's real list, revenue split, and subscription data together.
