The path a buyer actually takes

Following a cold research buyer from first click to repeat order. Green is working, amber is where revenue leaks today, blue is the system that closes it.

Working
Leak point
System we build

1. Lands on the site

A cold buyer arrives from search, social, or a referral and the signup popup fires immediately, covering the hero and the value story.

Leak

Instant popup interrupts before trust is built. A large share of cold visitors bounce straight back out.

System

Retime the capture to earn attention, so it appears after the visitor engages, not before they have read anything.

2. Browses products and pricing

Catalog, prices, and COAs are all public. No login required. The quality story is strong and visible.

Working. This is a real asset, not a problem.

3. Signs up or adds to cart, then hesitates

Research buyers compare vendors, testing data, and reputation before spending. Many enter an email or start a cart and leave to shop around.

Leak

No automated follow-up fires. The contact goes cold and comparison-shops a competitor who does follow up.

System

Speed-to-lead sequence within seconds, leading with COA and testing proof plus the membership offer, routed into a live pipeline.

4. Has a question at the buying moment

Purity, storage, shipping, membership terms, research-use guidelines. There is no live way to ask, so the question goes unanswered.

Leak

After-hours and pre-sale questions get no response. Silent bounce.

System

24/7 concierge that answers in compliant language and captures anyone not ready to buy.

5. Buys and gets the order

Next-day US shipping, quality delivered. The transaction experience is solid.

Working. Fulfillment is not the problem.

6. Finishes the vial, could reorder

Peptide research is a reorder category. Members and one-time buyers naturally cycle back, or should.

Leak

No lifecycle sequence to prompt the reorder, onboard members, or win back the lapsed. Recurring revenue sits uncollected.

System

Retention and reactivation engine that prompts reorders and revives quiet buyers automatically. No new ad spend.

7. Would vouch, never asked

Happy customers exist. The category is won on reviews and third-party trust signals.

Leak

Reviews are not being requested on purpose, so proof never compounds and lower-quality competitors can look more trusted.

System

Reputation engine that asks at the right moment and catches any unhappy signal privately first.
