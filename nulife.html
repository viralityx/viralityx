Where the revenue leaks

Six gaps between demand and dollars

Leak 01

The signup popup is doing the wrong job

It fires the instant a visitor lands, covering your hero and your value proposition before anyone has read a word. Aggressive popups like this collect a few emails but push a large share of cold visitors straight back to the tab they came from. Worse, the people who do enter their email and then leave without buying appear to get no automated follow-up. You are capturing contacts and then letting them go cold.

Fix = same capture, far less bounce, plus a follow-up loop on every signup
Leak 02

No speed-to-lead on new signups and abandoned carts

Research buyers do not impulse-buy. They compare vendors, read testing data, and check reputation before they spend. When someone signs up or adds to cart and does not check out, the window to win them is measured in minutes, not days. Without an automated sequence that fires immediately with your COA proof, testing standards, and membership offer, that buyer comparison-shops a competitor and does not come back.

This is the single largest pool of recoverable first-order revenue
Leak 03

Retention and reactivation are not automated

Research peptides are a reorder category. Customers run protocols, finish a vial, and buy again. That reorder should be prompted, not left to memory. There is no visible lifecycle sequence to onboard new members, nudge lapsed members before they churn, or win back one-time buyers who went quiet. This is the cheapest revenue you have because these people already trust you.

Reactivating existing buyers costs a fraction of acquiring new ones
Leak 04

No live answer for pre-sale and compliance questions

There is no chat to answer the questions that stop a purchase in the moment: purity, testing, COAs, shipping, storage, membership terms, research-use guidelines. A buyer with a question at 11pm has nowhere to ask, so they leave. A 24/7 concierge that answers in your compliant language and routes them to purchase or captures their contact turns silent bounces into conversations.

Every unanswered question at the buying moment is a lost order
Leak 05

Reputation is not being built on purpose

This entire category competes on trust signals: third-party testing, reviews, and social proof. Buyers cross-shop vendors on exactly this. Your quality is real, but if satisfied customers are not being systematically asked for a public review at the right moment, that proof never gets built and a lower-quality competitor with more reviews looks more trustworthy than you. Worth confirming on the call: what does your current review process look like.

Trust is the deciding factor in this vertical and it compounds
Leak 06

Wholesale and affiliate run on manual effort

Both channels exist, which is great, but a wholesale applicant or a new affiliate who does not get an instant, structured response cools off fast. These are high-value relationships. Speed and a clean onboarding flow are the difference between a partner who activates and one who ghosts.

Faster response and structured onboarding lifts partner activation
